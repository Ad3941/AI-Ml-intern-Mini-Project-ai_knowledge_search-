# Acme Corp Remote Work & Security Policy

## 1. Remote Work Guidelines
Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time. All remote workspaces must ensure privacy and prevent unauthorized viewing of sensitive company data.

## 2. Security Protocols
All company-issued laptops must use full-disk encryption (FileVault or BitLocker). Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of uppercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all internal SaaS applications.
